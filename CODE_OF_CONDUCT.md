# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

CDRS is a research community. We expect all contributors to engage with intellectual honesty, mutual respect, and a shared commitment to advancing the idea — not defending egos.

## Expected Behavior

- Engage with ideas, not people
- Cite sources and prior work accurately
- Accept and give critique constructively
- Credit others' contributions fairly
- Welcome contributors regardless of background or experience level

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks or harassment
- Dismissing contributions without substantive engagement
- Plagiarism or misattribution of ideas
- Gatekeeping based on credentials

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal from the repository.  
Report issues by opening a private issue labeled `conduct`.

---

*CDRS is built on the belief that open ideas, freely shared, produce better outcomes than closed ones. Act accordingly.*
