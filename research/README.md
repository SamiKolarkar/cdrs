# Research

This folder contains empirical evaluations, benchmark comparisons, and research findings related to CDRS.

---

## Open Research Questions

1. Does WH-question chaining improve decision quality over direct prompting or CoT?
2. Does constraint-based retrieval outperform text-embedding similarity for pattern matching?
3. What is the optimal stopping depth for recursive WHY decomposition?
4. Can importance weights be learned from outcomes rather than specified manually?
5. Does CDRS generalize across domains, or is it domain-specific?
6. How does CDRS compare to ReAct, Reflexion, and Tree of Thoughts?

---

## How to Contribute Research

1. Create a folder: `research/<your-finding-name>/`
2. Include your methodology, results, and interpretation
3. Open a Pull Request or Issue using the Research Finding template

Negative results are equally valuable and equally welcome.
